WHO WE ARE

Built for Farmers

VivasayaMart is a frontend agriculture marketplace concept designed to connect farmers and home gardeners with useful farming products in one simple platform.

From seeds and fertilizers to irrigation equipment and fresh produce, our goal is to make product discovery and online shopping easy.

Our Mission
